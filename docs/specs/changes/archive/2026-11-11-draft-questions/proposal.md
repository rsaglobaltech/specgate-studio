# Proposal: draft-questions

## Intent

The questions module of the brief (`docs/briefs/phase-2-specgate-flow.md`),
written down as requirements and scenarios a person can review before anything
is built: one place for every open question — the brief's, the drafts' and the
agent's — with what each blocks; answering it, and confirming that the
specification says the answer, all through specgate.

## Scope

In scope: what the brief says about questions — the list (REQ-091), the answer
written to its row with the requirement still blocked (REQ-092), "The spec says
it" lifting Needs Clarification (REQ-093).

Out of scope: every other module of the brief; answering the agent's question
inside Live (the build-live module); revising a requirement's text (the editor
and the drafts).
